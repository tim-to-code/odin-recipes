Lasagna

Home

Description

Lasagna is a layered Italian pasta dish made with flat sheets of pasta, meat sauce, béchamel and cheese. It is baked until the top turns golden and crisp.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 700ml tomato sauce
  • 500ml béchamel sauce
  • 200g grated parmesan

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook the ground beef with the tomato sauce for 20 minutes.
  3. Layer pasta, meat sauce and béchamel in a baking dish.
  4. Repeat until the dish is full, finishing with béchamel.
  5. Top with parmesan and bake for 40 minutes.
